Food rescue for canteens, messes and community kitchens

Turn surplus meals into someone's next meal.

AnnaSetu links college canteens and hostel messes with nearby NGOs and community kitchens. Donors post a surplus in under a minute; receivers see what is available, with clear safety guidance and pickup deadlines.

How it works

Three steps from surplus to a shared meal

1 {{ icon("plate") }}

Post the surplus

A canteen or mess lists the food, quantity, ready time, storage type and pickup window in under a minute.

2 {{ icon("donate") }}

Claim it

An NGO or community kitchen sees nearby listings, matched on distance, quantity and timing, and claims one.

3 {{ icon("truck") }}

Collect and deliver

A volunteer picks it up and delivers, and everyone follows live status: Posted, Claimed, Volunteer assigned, Collected, Delivered.
