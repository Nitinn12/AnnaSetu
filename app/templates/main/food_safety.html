Food safety

Food safety guidelines

Practical guidance for donors and receivers. This is general information, not a certification.

{{ icon("alert") }}

AnnaSetu does not certify or guarantee the safety of any food. Always follow your local food-safety rules and use your own judgement before serving or accepting food.

Storage

Storage types

{{ icon("leaf") }}

Ambient

Dry, sealed goods kept in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

{{ icon("thermometer") }}

Chilled

Keep at or below 5 °C where possible; use a cool box with ice packs for transport.

{{ icon("clock") }}

Frozen

Keep frozen until use; do not refreeze thawed food unless it was cooked first.

Timing

Time limits

Situation General guidance
Cooked food at room temperature Do not leave it out for more than 2 hours; reduce to 1 hour if the room is above 30 °C.
Cooling cooked food Cool and refrigerate promptly, in shallow containers, not large pots.
Reheating Heat until piping hot throughout; reheat only once.
Transport Keep chilled food cold in transit and collect before the pickup deadline on the listing.
Pickup deadline Collect only before the deadline on the listing. Do not collect or serve after it.

Labelling

Allergen labelling

Label every listing with the major allergens it may contain, and write 'unknown' when you are not sure.

Cereals containing gluten Crustaceans Eggs Fish Peanuts Soybeans Milk Tree nuts Celery Mustard Sesame seeds Sulphites Lupin Molluscs

Hygiene

Hygiene basics

Wash hands before handling food.

Use clean containers and lids.

Label each container with its preparation time.

Keep raw and cooked food apart.

Do not accept food in a damaged or open package when its history is unknown.
