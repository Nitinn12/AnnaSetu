About AnnaSetu

Food should reach the people who need it, not the bin.

AnnaSetu connects surplus food with the people and organisations who can put it to good use.

The context

The problem

Edible surplus from canteens and hostel messes is often thrown away at the end of service, while community kitchens and NGOs often lack timely access to it and a simple way to find it.

How it works

What AnnaSetu does

Donors post surplus with a quick form, describing what is available, how much there is and when it can be collected.

Receivers see listings matched on distance, quantity and timing, so they can claim what they can realistically collect.

Volunteers and everyone else follow live status as each donation moves through Posted, Claimed, Volunteer assigned, Collected and Delivered.
